How VendorOS Core sits on the other apps

VendorOS Core needs FOS Core, Contact Control Center, Documents and Collaboration. It works with Commerce, Training, Risk and Insights when they are on, and hides their tabs when they are off.

FOS Core→Contact Control Center→Documents→Collaboration→VendorOS Core→Vendor Portal

KPIs are a service, not a menu app

Each app registers its KPIs (name, query, target) with Insights. Dashboards, scorecards and the strip at the top of every workspace read them. Turn an app off and its KPIs disappear from the dashboards; the history stays.

Install a package

Installing registers the package switched off. An administrator turns it on, which runs its migrations. Inside FOS the default is the FOS tables, so there is one database and no copies.
